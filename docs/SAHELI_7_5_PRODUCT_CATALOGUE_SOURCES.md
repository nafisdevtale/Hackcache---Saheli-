## 1. Safety & Eligibility Charter

Every product admitted to the Saheli FLASH catalogue adheres to strict ethical and clinical safeguards:

- **Zero Prescription Medications:** No oral contraceptives, hormonal therapies, tranexamic acid, mefenamic acid, or prescription analgesics.
- **Zero Unverified Supplements:** No weight-loss supplements, detox teas, or unregulated herbal capsules.
- **Transparent Unit Pricing:** Display of total pack price and calculated per-unit cost in Indian Rupees (INR).
- **Explicit Contraindications & Warnings:** Every item includes specific handling, hygiene, and contraindication notes (e.g. TSS warning for internal cups, burn prevention for hot water bottles, proper dilution for disinfectants).
- **Real Indian Market Sourcing:** Grounded in real, accessible products from Jan Aushadhi Kendras, certified social enterprises (EcoFemme, Boondh), and widely available consumer brands across urban and rural India.
