## 2. Emergency Helplines & Triage Redirection

When user queries or logged symptoms meet emergency criteria (e.g., severe hemorrhage with dizziness/fainting, suspected ectopic pregnancy, severe acute pelvic pain, suicidal ideation), Saheli deterministically routes users to emergency care without algorithmic hesitation:

- **National Emergency Response Support System (ERSS):** **112**
- **Medical Emergency Ambulance Service:** **108**
- **Tele-MANAS Mental Health Crisis Line:** **14416**
- **KIRAN Mental Health Rehabilitation Helpline:** **1800-599-0019**
