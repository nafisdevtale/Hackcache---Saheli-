## 1. Overview & Clinical Evidence Charter

Saheli Discover is an educational library bridging the gap between accredited public health research and daily menstrual health realities. Every article in Saheli Discover adheres to three core standards:

1. **Accredited Public Health Evidence Base**: Every article directly synthesizes verified clinical and public health guidance (WHO, NHS UK, ACOG, FOGSI, UNICEF, MoHFW India).
2. **Transparent Evidence Attribution**: Saheli accurately cites published literature and guidelines, avoiding misleading claims of nonexistent institutional review boards or hospital ethics committee endorsements.
3. **Strict Non-Commercial Neutrality**: Zero sponsored content, zero brand endorsements, and zero claims promoting prescription drugs or unverified supplements.

## 3. Deep-Dive: When Supplies Are Unavailable

### Clinical Context

Across low- and middle-income settings, unexpected menstruation away from home, school supply stock-outs, or sudden natural displacement often force young girls and women into unsafe improvisations. In the absence of medical guidance, girls may insert improvised materials (old cloths, sofa foam, unsterilized cotton, raw sponges, newspaper) into the vaginal canal.

### Pathological Risks of Internal Improvisation

1. **Toxic Shock Syndrome (TSS)**: Insertion of non-biocompatible, unsterilized materials creates an anaerobic breeding ground for _Staphylococcus aureus_ or _Streptococcus pyogenes_, releasing superantigen toxins that can lead to rapid multi-organ failure.
2. **Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID)**: Bacterial ascent from contaminated cloths into the endometrium and fallopian tubes causes chronic pelvic pain and future tubal factor infertility.
3. **Vaginal Laceration & Foreign Body Retention**: Rough fibrous materials cause mucosal tears and can fragment, requiring surgical removal under anesthesia.

### Saheli's 4-Step Emergency Protocol

1. **Psychological Grounding**: Reassurance that unexpected bleeding is normal and not a moral failing.
2. **Confident Assistance**: Explicitly encouraging users to approach female teachers, school health nurses, colleagues, or community ASHA/ANM health workers who maintain emergency sanitary supplies.
3. **Safe Temporary EXTERNAL Measures**: If awaiting supplies, fold clean toilet paper or a clean cotton handkerchief into the underwear as a temporary **external barrier**.
4. **Strict Prohibition**: Bold, unambiguous warning never to insert any improvised object internally into the vagina.
