**Project:** Saheli — Your Cycle, Your Rhythm

**PR:** #1  
**Aesthetic Vision:** Modern Wellness Intelligence  
**Audit Date:** October 2026

---

### Executive Overview

The Saheli 9.1 renovation moved the frontend from fragmented, uneven legacy cards to a unified **Modern Wellness Intelligence** experience. Every user-facing screen was systematically redesigned with content-proportional sizing, semantic depth layering, micro-press tactile feedback, and strict responsive guarantees across 320px to 1920px viewports.
