# SAHELI 10.0 — PROJECT NOVA SCREEN REDESIGN MATRIX

## Page-by-Page Transformation & Architectural Improvements

**Project:** Saheli — Your Cycle, Your Rhythm  
**Codename:** Project Nova  
**Branch:** `feature/saheli-10-nova`  
**Date:** October 2026

---

### Executive Overview

Project Nova replaces the fragmented "card-wall" syndrome and repetitive styling of previous iterations with a purposeful, calm, editorial wellness architecture. This document details the exact before/after design transformations across all 15 routes of the Saheli application.

---

### Page-by-Page Comparison Matrix

More Hub** (`/more`)                   | Disjointed list of overflow links with inconsistent icons and lack of grouping.                                                          | 4-pillar hierarchical gateway matching the application navigation architecture: My Health, My Space, AI Specialists, Account & Preferences; fast access to discreet mode and emergency help.                                              | Uniform 2-column responsive grid on tablet/desktop; zero orphan items; $\ge 48\text{px}$ touch targets across all link rows.                        |
